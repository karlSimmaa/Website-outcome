1. Aesthetics – How does it look?
Contrast – Text is clear and easy to read.
Alignment – Everything is lined up neatly.
Proximity – Related items are close; unrelated items have space.
Consistency – Same fonts, colours, and styles across pages.
Balance – Not too crowded or too empty.
White Space – Enough empty space around text and images.
